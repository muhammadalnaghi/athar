# WM067 — Additional evidenced card purchases, fresh-source integration

## Goal
Reduce routine approval work only where the real winning parser can establish a completed card purchase. Reuse the accepted canonical PURCHASE admission/projection rather than creating another bookkeeping engine. This follows Master Brief WM029 and WM-01/19/21. It does not authorize rewriting old financial history or globally enabling automatic recording.

## Inputs and authority
The parser lane supplies `validated-card-purchase-066`, inserted after existing bank-specific templates and before the unchanged universal fallback. The wrapper must reuse that fallback's exact money/type extraction, retain its recognized-bank sender restriction, and require a formal purchase heading, separately labelled card evidence, one complete positive explicitly denominated principal, and no contradictory/composite movement. Only the actual winning wrapper plus a valid complete context may supply explicit posting authority. A forged result ID or generic completed-payment sentence cannot substitute for the context. A separate complete balance field is not spending and must not conceal another fee/currency.

## Integration
After inspecting the worker's owned diff and passing adversarial controls, add the single reviewed ID to the existing pure purchase mapper. Retain its SMS/notification namespace, positive exact money, genuine stated date, POSTED/EXPLICIT_POSTED requirements and all existing purchase/card-repayment behavior. Keep fresh-source admission, source binding, untouched-row/user-choice checks, atomic rollback, replay idempotence and opaque restore-session checks unchanged. Do not add an income/refund/transfer adapter or infer account ownership. Classification remains optional and cannot grant posting authority.

## Edges and acceptance
Missing dates/currency, additional principal/fee fields, unsupported currency extraction, authorization/hold/decline/schedule, merchant-field pseudo-headings, wallet funding, refund/reversal, withdrawal and transfer contexts remain outside automatic purchase recording. Preserve every previously bound JVM identity. Verify unchanged source economics outside an independently reviewed eligible source set; a metadata preflight or matching population delta is not independent monetary proof. Run one integrated candidate, then the exact new native source-bound path with encrypted baseline capture and one authenticated restoration. Reopen retained finances and confirm rollout OFF afterward. If the family has no eligible real sources, state that honestly instead of claiming queue reduction. Native startup comparison is a separate WM065 observation; neither scope establishes full 22-capability, phone, release, production AI or zero-bug acceptance.
